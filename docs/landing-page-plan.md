# Landing concept implementation

1. Preserve the refined hero and partner scene; give the hero assessment and services links.
2. Alternate desktop content: hero right, partners left, services right, sample project left, assessment right, about left, FAQs right, contact left.
3. Use illustrative services and explicitly fictional project content. No invented testimonials or confirmed partner claims.
4. Build an in-memory assessment: business profile, readiness questions, required email, illustrative result. No persistence, network submission, or email delivery.
5. Extend a decorative whale journey through reserved space opposite the copy; mobile stacks whale space and copy. Reduced motion shows static section whales.
6. Verify navigation, required fields, back/restart flows, score gate, responsive spacing, reduced motion, build and lint.

This is a review prototype, not an approved service catalogue or validated scoring model. Production needs confirmed copy, partner permissions, scoring criteria, privacy terms and delivery infrastructure.
